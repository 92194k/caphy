Offline / Local Mode
Keep using CAPHY when there's no internet
CAPHY normally works from anywhere over the internet. If the internet goes down, it can still run in Local Mode — your phone talks straight to this laptop over your local Wi‑Fi, with no internet needed. This only works when your phone and this laptop are on the same Wi‑Fi network (or the same hotspot), so it naturally can't happen while you're away from home — that's by design.

Local addresshttp://192.168.1.3:5000

CONNECT PHONE & LAPTOP OFFLINE — STEP BY STEP
  1. Put this laptop and your phone on the same Wi‑Fi. No internet? Turn on a phone hotspot and connect the laptop to it, or use any router even without an internet uplink.
  2. Keep CAPHY running on this laptop (this window).
  3. Open the CAPHY app on your phone. It switches by itself when there's no internet but this laptop is reachable — you'll see an amber "Offline mode · Local Wi‑Fi" banner.
  4. That's it — live view and controls work over the local network.
